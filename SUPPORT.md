# Getting help

- **Docs:** [offrun.dev/docs](https://offrun.dev/docs/), including [Troubleshooting](https://offrun.dev/docs/troubleshooting/).
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/founderly-inc/offrun/issues/new/choose) with the bug form.
- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/founderly-inc/offrun/discussions).
- **Email:** [hello@offrun.dev](mailto:hello@offrun.dev).

Before you paste logs or screenshots, remove email addresses, tokens, keys and the names of private repositories. To send us your log files privately instead, use Send logs in Settings, General; Offrun cleans them before they leave your computer.
