# Security

Please report a security problem in Offrun privately, by email to [hello@offrun.dev](mailto:hello@offrun.dev), and not in a public issue or discussion.

Tell us the Offrun version (Settings, About), your system, what you found and how to reproduce it. We will reply to confirm we have it, and tell you when it is fixed.

Our contact is also published at [offrun.dev/.well-known/security.txt](https://offrun.dev/.well-known/security.txt).
